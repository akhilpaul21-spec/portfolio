## How I Think About Product Design

Every initiative is evaluated across four dimensions:

### Customer Impact
- Does it solve a meaningful customer problem?
- Does it improve usability, adoption, or satisfaction?

### Business Impact
- Does it support revenue growth, retention, or market expansion?

### Operational Impact
- Does it reduce complexity for customers and internal teams?

### Strategic Impact
- Does it strengthen the platform's long-term competitive advantage?

The goal is not only to create usable experiences but also to help organizations make better decisions and build sustainable competitive advantages.
